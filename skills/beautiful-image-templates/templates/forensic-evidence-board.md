# Forensic Evidence Board

Use when the subject is about claims, evidence, examples, contradictions, or pattern discovery.

## Visual System

- Structure: evidence clusters connected by threads
- Composition: central claim, surrounding exhibits, annotated links, restrained density
- Style terms: old photograph, annotated evidence board, pins, threads, muted color, paper
- Palette: cork, paper, graphite, muted red thread
- Text policy: very short exhibit labels; avoid sensational words

## Prompt Template

```text
Create a restrained forensic evidence board image about {subject}. Show a central claim surrounded by evidence clusters, clipped examples, annotated relationships, pins, and thin thread connections. Use old photograph texture, muted color, paper fragments, careful investigative composition, unsensational tone. No crime-board melodrama, no conspiracy aesthetic, no blood red overload, no unreadable paragraphs.
```

## Good Fit

- Investigations
- Evaluating examples against a thesis
- Showing evidence rather than just summarizing conclusions
