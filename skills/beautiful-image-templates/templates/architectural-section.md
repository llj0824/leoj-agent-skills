# Architectural Section

Use when the subject has layers: foundations, load-bearing ideas, circulation paths, and visible surface outcomes.

## Visual System

- Structure: cross-section or cutaway diagram
- Composition: side view, stacked levels, labels on structural parts, thin measurement marks
- Style terms: architectural, blueprint, cross-section, matte, side view, technical drawing
- Palette: off-white, graphite, blueprint blue, restrained accent
- Text policy: labels for levels and structural parts; avoid long prose in-image

## Prompt Template

```text
Create an architectural section image about {subject}. Show the idea as a cutaway structure with foundations, load-bearing concepts, circulation paths, upper-level consequences, and visible surface outcomes. Use architectural blueprint style, precise cross-section drawing, side view, matte paper, thin measurement lines, restrained technical labels, quiet professional tone. No decorative building fantasy, no photorealistic skyscraper, no cluttered card layout, no glossy render.
```

## Good Fit

- Layered conceptual systems
- Product or organization structure
- Interviews where foundations matter more than anecdotes
