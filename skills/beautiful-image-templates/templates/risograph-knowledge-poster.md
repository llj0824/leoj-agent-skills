# Risograph Knowledge Poster

Use when the image should feel editorial, tactile, and alive while still carrying real information structure.

## Visual System

- Structure: poster with blocks, diagram fragments, and a strong visual hierarchy
- Composition: one dominant shape, 2-3 secondary blocks, overprint texture, clear reading order
- Style terms: risograph, two-color overprint, imperfect registration, paper, graphic poster
- Palette: two inks plus paper, usually red/black, blue/orange, or green/black
- Text policy: large headings only; exact words should be overlaid

## Prompt Template

```text
Create a risograph knowledge poster about {subject}. Use a strong poster composition with one dominant abstract shape, 2-3 structured information blocks, diagram fragments, clear hierarchy, two-color overprint, imperfect registration, tactile paper texture, editorial energy. Keep the information legible and purposeful. No glossy gradients, no fake app dashboard, no tiny text, no chaotic collage.
```

## Good Fit

- Public-facing explainers
- Editorial newsletters or social visuals
- Topics that need energy without becoming shallow
