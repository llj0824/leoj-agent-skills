# Ink Constellation

Use when the subject is associative, idea-dense, or reflective, and the image should feel spacious rather than diagram-heavy.

## Visual System

- Structure: central thesis with satellite ideas
- Composition: one central mark, orbiting nodes, thin connective lines, large blank space
- Style terms: Japanese ink, constellation map, paper, generous blank space, quiet annotations
- Palette: black ink, warm paper, optional muted red seal accent
- Text policy: very sparse labels; exact text belongs in overlay

## Prompt Template

```text
Create a minimal ink constellation image about {subject}. Place the central thesis as an abstract ink form with satellite idea nodes around it, connected by thin constellation lines and quiet annotations. Use Japanese ink, warm paper texture, generous blank space, elegant asymmetry, calm intellectual mood. No decorative star fantasy, no crowded mind map, no neon, no motivational quote typography.
```

## Good Fit

- Philosophical or literary subjects
- Associative interviews
- Elegant article images where silence is part of the design
