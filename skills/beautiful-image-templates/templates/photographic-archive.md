# Photographic Archive

Use when the image should feel concrete, observational, and materially grounded.

## Visual System

- Structure: archival photograph with restrained annotation
- Composition: one real-feeling subject, surrounding context, subtle margin notes or catalog marks
- Style terms: old photograph, film style, natural lighting, paper, wide view
- Palette: natural muted color, sepia, black-and-white, or low-purity color
- Text policy: minimal caption marks; use overlay for exact captions

## Prompt Template

```text
Create a photographic archive image about {subject}. Make it feel like a real archival photograph or catalog plate: concrete subject presence, natural lighting, film style, subtle paper grain, restrained margin marks, observational wide view, credible material detail. No stock-photo polish, no overdramatic blur, no glossy advertising look, no fake unreadable captions.
```

## Good Fit

- People, places, objects, historical scenes
- Brand or product visuals that need concrete presence
- Article images where realism carries trust
