# Museum Wall Chart

Use when the subject has lineage: history, intellectual inheritance, branching schools, or a chronology of ideas.

## Visual System

- Structure: gallery wall timeline with branches and archival labels
- Composition: horizontal timeline, clusters, framed fragments, clear hierarchy
- Style terms: old photograph, archival labels, warm gallery lighting, paper, museum wall
- Palette: warm white, black ink, sepia, muted accent
- Text policy: short plaque-style labels; exact dates should be added deterministically

## Prompt Template

```text
Create a museum wall chart image about {subject}. Show the lineage as a restrained gallery timeline with branching idea families, archival fragments, small plaque labels, and clear chronological flow. Use old photograph texture, paper, warm gallery lighting, restrained serif typography, archival design, calm scholarly mood. No conspiracy wall, no sensational pins, no crowded paragraphs, no glossy corporate infographic.
```

## Good Fit

- Intellectual history
- Concept evolution
- Expert interviews with references and branching traditions
